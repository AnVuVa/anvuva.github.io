I am a third-year Computer Science student at Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST), a Research Assistant at BKAI Lab, and co-founder of Hibiscus Co. My work focuses on computer vision for biotechnology and biometrics, including spatial transcriptomics, endoscopy, and fingerprint recognition.

Beyond computer vision, I have experience in software engineering, AI product development, and competitive programming. I also work with NLP, RAG systems, agentic AI, and evolutionary algorithms.
